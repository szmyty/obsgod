# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Review the work with concrete
evidence, explain disagreements and respect personal boundaries. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory abuse, publication of another person's private
information and repeated disruptive behavior do not belong in project spaces.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces
maintained for this project. Maintainers may remove disruptive content or limit
participation to protect contributors. This document does not promise a response
time, confidential investigation infrastructure or moderation outside spaces the
maintainers control.

Do not post private incident details or another person's personal information in
a public issue. If no private maintainer contact is currently listed, use a
minimal issue requesting a private contact route, without identifying affected
people or publishing the incident. The owner must establish a maintained private
conduct contact before claiming broader community support infrastructure.

Security vulnerability reports use the separate [security process](SECURITY.md).
