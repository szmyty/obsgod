Make the stream yours.

Profiles, local clips and separate audio choices share the managed OBS state. Confirm the result before relying on it.

Stream profiles

Choose a bounded preset, preview the effective values, then save a named profile. Per-game bindings are suggestions for the next explicit cold Start or local output; changing games does not automatically restart output or change encoder settings. Destination credentials stay local and are not part of a portable nonsecret profile.

A preset is a starting point. Resolution, frame rate and encoder settings affect game performance, temperature, bandwidth and clip size. Make a local clip and measure your Deck before broadcasting. Keep a known working profile for recovery.

Between-game screen

Game-only capture can keep output running while showing the selected black screen, text, local image or local looping video. Import media through Desktop Setup while outputs are stopped. Use material you have rights to broadcast. A missing game, ambiguous game or unsupported capture format must not expose the full screen as a fallback.

This behavior needs actual Gamescope privacy tests on the candidate version. A generated placeholder or successful import does not prove which screen your viewers receive.

Recording and replay

Record locally and Start replay buffer do not broadcast. Each output has its own confirmed state. Stop recording finalizes a clip; it does not stop an existing stream. Save replay before stopping the buffer to preserve it. Cancel recording preserves its partial MKV.

Storage preferences include the recording directory, bounded replay duration/memory and a free-space reserve. Low space can stop local outputs while preserving streaming and finalized media. A host write check does not prove OBS Flatpak access to a custom directory.

The local library indexes finalized managed files. Export preserves the original. Retention cleanup requires a preview and explicit selection while output is idle; it is not background deletion. Preserve interrupted or changed files and inspect them in Desktop Mode. Default recordings remain in the XDG Videos OBSGod directory.

Game sound, microphone and headsets

Desktop audio and microphone are separate. Microphone starts opt-in and a selected output monitor can include sound from other applications: game-only video is not per-application audio isolation.

The Steam Deck has built-in microphones. The LCD model can disable its built-in microphone when the 3.5 mm jack is used; a built-in, analog, USB or Bluetooth route must be checked on the actual device. A device appearing in a selector is not proof that OBS receives it. Do not silently substitute another microphone if the selected route disappears.

Under Live audio & microphone, enabling microphone capture starts muted. A device change forces mute. Mute microphone on stream and Unmute microphone on stream use observed state; the saved preference is separate. Game/system and microphone gains are bounded to 0–200% and applied explicitly. A disconnected or unconfirmed route needs attention; it must not be presented as confirmed muted or healthy. A meter, command acknowledgement or notification does not prove audible recorded or viewer sound.

Optional hold-to-talk is a focused panel control, using A, Enter, Space or a pointer after explicitly arming. It is not a global in-game chord. Releasing, losing focus or leaving the panel ends the request; if OBS control is lost, mute cannot be verified. Stop output and check OBS rather than assuming privacy.

  1. Record a local clip with game sound and speech.
  2. Mute the microphone, speak again, then unmute and test once more.
  3. Listen to the finalized clip. Confirm only the intended source changed.
  4. Repeat when plugging/unplugging a headset, changing output or resuming from sleep; explicitly choose a supported route if recovery is needed.

Health and recovery

Observed output health can report progress, reconnecting/stalled output and capture/audio warnings. Unknown state may still be live. Inspect OBS and the channel; keep recovery Stop available. Automatic local route recovery must not start a stopped broadcast.

Keep support diagnostics filtered: never include stream keys, OBS passwords, private setup URLs, full local media paths or unrelated application information. A support bundle is reviewable evidence, not a guarantee of viewer reception.
