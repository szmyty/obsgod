Inspect first

From the installed or extracted reviewed package, preview the removal inventory:

python3 uninstall.py --dry-run

Actual removal must refuse active, unknown or busy output. Review the paths and explicit confirmation. Ordinary OBS, other plugins and shared dependencies are outside this tool's owned-data scope.

Choose the intended scope

CommandIntended effect in the candidate source
python3 uninstall.pyRemove the app and its Decky plugin through the supported local route. Preserve settings, state, cache, backups and media by default.
python3 uninstall.py --resetKeep the app/plugin installed and clear owned configuration, private managed OBS configuration, state and cache.
python3 uninstall.py --wipeRemove the app/plugin plus owned configuration, state, cache, backups and owned application data. Media remains a separate choice.
python3 uninstall.py --purge-settingsLegacy option: uninstall and remove settings/private managed OBS configuration without implying a complete state/backup wipe.

These are source-preview commands. Consult python3 uninstall.py --help from your exact installed package; older previews may expose fewer options.

Recordings require a separate choice

Recordings stay unless explicitly selected. Consult the exact candidate's --delete-recordings and repeatable --recording PATH help, then inspect the dry-run inventory before confirming. Changed, unrelated and unselected files must be preserved. A custom or shared recording directory is not an application-owned tree to remove recursively.

If an installation is broken or Decky's local removal route is unavailable, use the reported recovery step. The tool must not replace that failure with broad privileged directory deletion.
