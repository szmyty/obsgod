# Decky Stream brief

Status: **01 / aurora creative direction selected by the owner**. Candidate
vector/raster masters and exports are implemented for source-preview review;
rights/license treatment, production adoption and device acceptance remain open.

Decky Stream makes local OBS controls available in Steam Deck's Quick Access
panel. The core remains usable without payment or a service account. It controls
a graphical OBS installation through its authenticated local WebSocket; it is
not OBS, Decky Loader or Valve software and is not an official product of those
projects. Its release support claims must match actual hardware qualification.

Selected user-facing name and source-preview display: **deckystream**. Intended repository slug:
**deckystream** (owner preference updated 2026-11-14 from `decky-stream`).
The owner prefers lowercase, single-word naming. The selected artwork batch
applies display styling while preserving installed compatibility identifiers.
Current repository: https://github.com/szmyty/obsgod . CLI/storage/plugin IDs stay
`obsgod`/`OBSGod` until an independently qualified compatibility migration.

Primary audience: Steam Deck users configuring privately in Desktop Mode and
controlling streaming, recording, replay and microphone choices in Gaming Mode.
Priorities: clear observed output state, safe stopped-state changes, privacy,
usable controller targets, legible text and explicit recovery.

## Selected direction: orbital stream, 01 / aurora

The owner locked **01 / aurora** as the primary creative direction on 2026-11-14:
the open orbital ring and flowing S-shaped stream ribbon, with a luminous
aqua/cyan-to-violet gradient, controlled depth/glow and a near-black field.
The aqua/violet finish retains the original board's airy, restrained atmosphere.
The selected comparison image, exact source hashes and approval scope are recorded
in [the direction selection](candidates/concepts/selection.json).

Keep the original orbital-stream board and square gradient icon as concepts and
references. Retain 02 / afterglow, 03 / solar and 04 / pearl as alternative
concepts; they are not the selected primary palette. The earlier mint-signal art
and existing generated kit remain historical preparation, not the implementation
of this newly selected direction. Do not overwrite that artwork or relabel old
generation/check receipts.

The PNG concepts do not establish identical vector geometry, approved master
files or final export dimensions. Next, author the actual selected source and
check its small-size rendering and target use. Decky's JSX icon supports color
and gradients; a monochrome treatment is an optional legibility fallback, not a
platform requirement. Keep observed stream-state cues distinct from decorative
logo colors and preserve native Decky behavior.

The mark must not imply affiliation, replace status labels, use third-party
logos or depend on motion. This selection does not set an organization-wide
palette, approve rights/licensing, accept final vector/export output, qualify
devices or publish a release. Actual organization, public contact, rights/license
treatment (including AI assistance disclosure), domain and release destinations
remain separate facts and decisions before production adoption.
