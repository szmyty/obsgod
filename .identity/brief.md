# Decky Stream brief

Status: proposed creative direction; owner review required.

Decky Stream makes local OBS controls available in Steam Deck's Quick Access
panel. The core remains usable without payment or a service account. It controls
a graphical OBS installation through its authenticated local WebSocket; it is
not OBS, Decky Loader or Valve software and is not an official product of those
projects. Its release support claims must match actual hardware qualification.

Selected user-facing name: **Decky Stream**. Intended slug: **decky-stream**.
Current repository: https://github.com/szmyty/obsgod . CLI/storage/plugin IDs stay
`obsgod`/`OBSGod` until an independently qualified compatibility migration.

Primary audience: Steam Deck users configuring privately in Desktop Mode and
controlling streaming, recording, replay and microphone choices in Gaming Mode.
Priorities: clear observed output state, safe stopped-state changes, privacy,
usable controller targets, legible text and explicit recovery.

The draft mark uses original simple geometry: a play triangle and a transmission
arc inside a rounded field. It must not imply affiliation, replace state labels,
use third-party logos, or depend on motion. Proposed mint/ink colors reflect the
existing documentation preview; they do not establish an organizational palette.
Keep native Decky theme and icon conventions in the live plugin.

Owner must approve the creative direction, actual organization, public contact,
rights/license treatment (including AI assistance disclosure), domain and release
destinations before this draft becomes a production brand kit.
