Status: **Working name accepted; final identity and migration proposed**.
The owner selected “Decky Stream” and requested a future `decky-stream` identity,
while leaving organization/final brand choices open. `product.json` records the

## Context and choice

Existing users have `obsgod` commands, an `OBSGod` plugin, private XDG data,
updater trust rules and immutable downloads. Treating a label as a storage/plugin/
repository migration risks lost credentials, duplicate plugins and broken updates.
Keep compatibility identifiers while preparing a reviewed transition.

Use the Identity consumer framework to bootstrap practical product assets. Those
assets need their own actual source/rights facts, owner creative approval and
generated-output evidence. A draft logo/manifest grants no trademark or ownership.

## Pending decisions and consequences

Organization/canonical repository, final name/mark, domain, publisher and creative
acceptance remain owner inputs. Preparation here does not create accounts,
transfer a repository, change another project, publish or deploy. Migration
details stay proposed until reviewed against exact old/new trust and data paths.
Keep original source/dependency attribution; a new name does not rewrite ownership.
