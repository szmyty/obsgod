Local OBS connection

The managed instance uses an authenticated local control connection at 127.0.0.1:4457. Its generated password is separate from your stream key. Normal OBS scenes and passwords are separate.

Change or recover the local control password

First use Close idle OBS / apply settings above. If authentication is broken, inspect and close the managed OBS window manually. Password changes are refused while OBS or its listener might be active.

A change updates both private managed settings. Afterwards, open managed OBS and test the connection to verify reconnection. Generating a password keeps it private; you do not need to copy it.

Secrets are stored as private local files, including OBS's own service settings. Never share those files or screenshots of your stream key.
